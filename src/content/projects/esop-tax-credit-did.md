As part of a four-person team, I worked on estimating the causal effect of Colorado's employee-ownership tax credit on firm-level ESOP participation and asset accumulation.

We compared Colorado with Utah using difference-in-differences, and then built a synthetic Colorado from other states as a robustness check.
